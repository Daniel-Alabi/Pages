Factor Blaster

Numbers are falling. Your cannon fires primes.

Hit a number with one of its factors and it divides. Divide it all the way down to 1 and it's gone. Fire a prime that doesn't divide it and the shot bounces off.

Move with ← → or A D, fire with space, switch primes with 1 2 3 4.
