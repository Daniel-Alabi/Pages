Built around your eyes

Optics first. Everything else second.

Most headsets are computers with lenses bolted on. Prism One was engineered the other way around — the optical path was locked first, and every component earned its place behind it.

Pancake lens stack

A folded three-element optic that's edge-to-edge sharp, with 40% less glass between you and the world. No sweet spot to hunt for — the whole lens is the sweet spot.

Eye-tracked rendering

Foveated rendering paints full detail exactly where you're looking and saves power everywhere else. You never notice it working — that's the point.

Open spatial audio

Off-ear drivers place sound in the room, not in your head. You stay aware of your space while the soundstage stays exactly where the world puts it.
