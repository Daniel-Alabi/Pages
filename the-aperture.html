Gate N · Roof System

The iris roof

Twelve ETFE petals rotate on a steel compression ring, opening the pitch to sun and rain between fixtures and sealing the bowl on match day. Drive the mechanism below.

Plan · roof level Scale 1:2000
Status · Sealed for match day
  • Petals12 ETFE-clad blades on a recycled-steel compression ring — each drives independently, so partial shade configurations are possible on hot days.
  • CycleFull open or close in under 15 minutes; wind-rated to operate up to 60 km/h and lock down beyond it.
  • SkinPhotovoltaic film laminated to the upper ETFE layer feeds on-site batteries covering roughly 70% of match-day demand.
  • AcousticsClosed configuration reflects crowd noise back into the bowl; open configuration vents concert sound ceilings for neighbouring districts.
Gate E · Section

One cut through the building

Everything that makes the stadium work stacks in a single section: the timber diagrid, the steep bowl, the open concourse, and the rail station buried in the podium.

Section A–A · looking north Scale 1:1250
1

Timber–steel diagrid

Glulam lattice on recycled-steel nodes carries the roof ring — cutting embodied carbon about 40% against an all-steel shell and giving the facade warmth at street level.

2

360° open concourse

The mid-tier concourse stays visually open to the pitch, so no one loses sight of the game while ordering ahead to seat-adjacent pickup lockers.

3

Slide-out pitch tray

Hybrid grass lives on a rolling tray with sub-surface heating, aeration, and moisture sensing feeding an AI groundskeeping model.

4

Rail under the podium

An integrated rail/metro station sits directly beneath the entry podium — transit-first access with minimal parking and a 5,000-cycle bike hub.

Gate S · Pitch Operations

48 hours to a concert floor

The grass never suffers for the business plan. The full playing surface rolls out to a sunlit grow yard on rails, leaving a rigid event deck behind — the conversion that keeps an international venue solvent between fixtures.

  • SurfaceHybrid natural-synthetic turf, stitched for international-standard traction and recovery.
  • ConversionPitch out, deck configured, and rigging up inside 48 hours — in either direction.
  • Grow yardOutside the bowl the tray sits under open sky with irrigation drawn from harvested rainwater.

Gate L · Legacy Plan

Designed to shrink

The most futuristic thing a tournament stadium can do is refuse to become a ruin. The upper tier is a bolted, demountable structure — its removal is part of the original engineering, not an afterthought.

Phase 1 · Tournament mode
65,000

Full three-tier bowl for international fixtures, finals, and opening ceremonies. Upper tier carried on independent bolted steelwork.

Phase 2 · Legacy mode
45,000

Upper tier demounted and re-erected elsewhere as a mid-size venue. The roof ring, facade, and services are sized for the smaller bowl from day one.
